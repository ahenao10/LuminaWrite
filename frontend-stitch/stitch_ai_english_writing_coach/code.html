Vocabulary & Collocation Paragraph 1, Line 3
check_circle
close lead to big burnout
arrow_forward
check culminate in severe burnout

Pedagogical Insight: In scholarly and analytical prose, phrases like "lead to big" sound overly colloquial and vague. The phrasal verb "culminate in" precisely denotes reaching a decisive climactic or consequential point after a long progression. Pairing it with the adjective "severe" establishes an academic tone typical of CEFR C1/B2+ registers.

Recommended synonyms: precipitate intense exhaustion induce acute fatigue
Lexical Sophistication Paragraph 2, Line 1

Pedagogical Insight: Overusing generic causative verbs ("makes someone feel") alongside generic intensifiers ("very alone") dilutes analytical authority. The transitive verb "engender" formalizes the causality, and "pervasive isolation" accurately paints widespread systemic detachment.

Grammatical Syntax Paragraph 2, Line 3

arrow_forward

Pedagogical Insight: In English, purpose is designated via the full infinitive construction (to + base verb), rather than for + base verb. Alternatively, one could construct "for the purpose of mitigating" or "in order to mitigate", but the simple infinitive "to mitigate" is concise and grammatically sound.
